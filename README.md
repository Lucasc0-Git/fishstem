FISHSTEM

This is an experimental idea for a game, and I'm not sure what will happen to this project. Any opinion is welcome.

Description:

- You are *someone* whose job is to spread life into a deep prehistoric ocean, you earn DNA points from cleaning the location, and you have passive income from every flora and fauna from the location.
- 2D, relaxing atmosphere
- You can give me some more ideas! As I said, the idea is just experimental, so any personal opinions are very welcome!

The name is a contraction of "fish" and "ecosystem"
